Frequently Asked Questions

Kya tax return file karne ke baad advance payment karni hogi?

Bilkul nahi! Hamari policy hai ke pehle aap ka kaam FBR portal par mukammal hoga. Jab aap apna naam Active Taxpayer List (ATL) mein dekh lenge, uske baad hi payment li jayegi.

Mujhe filer banne ke kya faiday hain?

Filer banne par aap bank cash withdrawal, gari ki khareed-o-farokht, property transfer, aur prize bonds par withholding tax se adha (50%) ya mukammal bach jatay hain. Non-filers ko double aur triple tax dena parta hai.

Kya 2026 ki Tax Return file karna lazmi hai?

Jee haan, FBR ke naye rules ke mutabiq 2026 ki tax return file na karne walon par bhari jurmana (penalties), mobile SIM blocking, aur foreign travel par pabandi jese iqdamaat shamil hain.

Overseas Pakistanis ke liye kya rules hain?

Overseas Pakistanis ko foreign remittance par tax ki chhoot (exemption) hoti hai. Hum unki return zero tax par file karke unhein Pakistan mein property aur bank transactions
